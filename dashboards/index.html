Data Engineering & Leakage Mitigation Strategy

  • Deduplication: 39 exact duplicate entries removed from raw ingestion.
  • Missing Value Discovery: Audited that missing demographic entries (685 rows) and review turnaround duration (691 rows) occupied non-overlapping cohorts, resolved via complete case analysis.
  • Target Leakage Mitigation: Isolated physician reviewer text narratives (Findings) from tabular models because post-hoc summaries contained verbatim outcome statements (e.g., "Therefore, the Health Plan denial should be upheld").
  • Feature Representation: Mapped patient age into 7 ordered lifecycle bands (0–6), and applied high-cardinality label encodings to 532 diagnosis subcategories and 498 treatment classifications.
